4. How to Use d'Alba Vita Toning Mist Serum

🌿 Simple Routine

  • Step 1 — Cleanse: Wash with a gentle cleanser and pat dry.
  • Step 2 — Toner (optional): Use your usual toner or skip it.
  • Step 3 — Spray serum: Shake lightly, close your eyes and mist 2–3 pumps from 20–30cm away. Pat in.
  • Step 4 — Moisturize: Follow with cream; add SPF in the morning.
  • During the day: Mist lightly over makeup when skin feels tight or dull.

8. d'Alba Vita Toning Mist Serum FAQ

What is d'Alba Vita Toning Mist Serum 8%?

It's a spray-type serum from Korean brand d'Alba (달바). The bottle reads 'Vita Toning Anti-Aging Serum 8%' and 'Anti-Aging Spray'. The Korean official-store set we reviewed is two 70ml bottles plus a gift bag.

Is it a mist or a serum?

Both, in practice. It sprays like a fine facial mist, but d'Alba positions it as a serum, so it's meant to replace or layer with your serum step rather than act as plain water mist.

How do I use it?

Hold it about 20-30cm from the face, close your eyes and spray 2-3 pumps after cleansing or toner, then pat in. You can also mist it over makeup during the day.

Is it different from the d'Alba Vita Toning 3-piece set?

Yes. The 3-piece set is a toner, serum and capsule cream. This product is the separate Vita Toning spray serum in a 70ml mist bottle.
