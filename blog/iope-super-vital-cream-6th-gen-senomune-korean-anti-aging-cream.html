Ask Korean shoppers to name a "gift cream" for parents, partners or themselves, and IOPE Super Vital Cream is one of the first gold jars that comes to mind. IOPE is one of Korea's best-known prestige skincare brands, and the Super Vital line has been its flagship anti-aging range for years.

The newest version is the 6th generation. According to the brand, it gathers 18 years of Super Vital know-how and introduces Senomune™, which IOPE presents as a next-generation aging-care ingredient. The brand claims that skin-aging indicators improve after just a single use.

"Eighteen years, six generations, one gold jar: the NEW Super Vital Cream is IOPE's latest answer to Korea's demand for rich yet comfortable anti-aging care."

1. What Is New in the 6th Generation

Each Super Vital generation has refreshed the formula while keeping the line's signature gold packaging. For the 6th generation, the headline is Senomune, positioned by IOPE as an entirely new dimension of anti-aging skincare.

  • Senomune™: The new aging-care ingredient at the center of the 6th-generation formula.
  • 18 Years of Know-How: The cream builds on nearly two decades of Super Vital research by IOPE.
  • Brand-Reported Popularity: IOPE's Korean product page highlights a #1 repurchase ranking, 100% customer satisfaction and approximately 3.5 million units sold cumulatively (brand-reported figures).

2. Texture & Everyday Feel

Korean reviewers describe the cream as thick and rich, yet it absorbs quickly without stickiness. Used as the last step at night, it leaves skin feeling moist the next morning, and makeup sits smoothly over it. Users also note smoother-looking skin with less flaking, and many add an extra layer on the eye area and smile lines. The scent is subtle rather than heavy.

4. How to Use

✨ Night Routine with Super Vital Cream

  • Step 1 — Cleanse & Tone: Start with clean, lightly hydrated skin.
  • Step 2 — Serum (Optional): Layer your usual serum first.
  • Step 3 — Cream as the Last Step: Warm a small scoop between your fingertips and press it over the face.
  • Step 4 — Extra Layer: Add a thin second layer on the eye area and smile lines.
