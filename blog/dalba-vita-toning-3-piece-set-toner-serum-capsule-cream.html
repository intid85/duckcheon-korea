d'Alba Vita Toning 3-Piece Set: Toner, Vita-Capsule Serum and Capsule Cream for a 2-Week Dark-Spot Routine

Published on October 28, 2026 · By DUCKCHEON KOREA Curation Team · Seoul, Republic of Korea · 6 Min Read

d'Alba is a Korean skincare brand best known for its bright, golden packaging and mist-style serums. Its Vita Toning line focuses on one job: helping uneven tone, dark spots and visible pores look better over a set period of use.

In Korea, the line is sold as a "2-week dark-spot care program" (2주 잡티 개선 프로그램) in a 3-piece set: toner 180ml, serum 100ml and a jar-type capsule cream 55g, plus a gift bag. The set is registered in Korea as a whitening (brightening) functional cosmetic.

"Three steps, one goal: the Vita Toning set is built for shoppers who want to focus on dark spots and pores for a fixed two-week stretch."

1. What's in the Set

  • Toner (180ml): The first step after cleansing, to prep skin for the serum.
  • Serum (100ml): Contains vita capsules that burst as you spread it, then sink into the skin.
  • Capsule Cream (55g jar): A thick cream that the brand says cares for pores and dark spots together.
  • Gift Bag: Included in the featured Korean set.

2. Texture & Everyday Feel

Korean users describe the serum's capsules popping as it absorbs, and the cream as thick but not sticky. Skin feels smooth after application, and makeup sits well the next morning. After about two weeks of consistent use, users report that dark-spot areas looked lighter and pores looked a little tighter. These are individual user impressions, not clinical results, and results vary from person to person.

4. How to Use

✨ 3-Step Vita Toning Routine

  • Step 1 — Toner: After cleansing, pat the toner over the face.
  • Step 2 — Serum: Spread the serum and let the vita capsules burst and absorb.
  • Step 3 — Capsule Cream: Finish with a thin layer of cream to seal everything in.
  • Tip: Use it every day for two weeks, and wear sunscreen in the daytime as part of any brightening routine.
