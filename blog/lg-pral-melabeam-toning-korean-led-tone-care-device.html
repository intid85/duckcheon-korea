1. LG Pra.L Melabeam Toning Review: Overview

This LG Pra.L Melabeam Toning review looks at the newest Korean at-home LED tone-care device from LG Pra.L, the beauty-device line of LG H&H. It launched in Korea in June 2026 with a clear brief: help even out dull, uneven-looking skin tone and dark spots at home, as part of a daily skincare routine.

In Korea, LG Pra.L is best known for its LED masks, and Melabeam Toning is the brand's first straight, pen-shaped device built around tone care. Korean shoppers use it at night after toner and serum, the same slot where they would put a brightening ampoule.

"A 190 g, pen-shaped Korean LED device with yellow, red and infrared light, EP and microcurrent — built for a daily five-minute tone-care step."

3. How to Use It

  1. Cleanse and wipe with a toner pad.
  2. Apply a skincare layer — LG pairs it with its GLASSLIKE Triple Toning Shot Ampoule, but a slippery serum or gel works for glide.
  3. Start at level 1 on your first use.
  4. Glide for about 5 minutes per area, keeping the tip flat on the skin. Add more product if the head starts to drag.
  5. Once a day, 1–2 modes max, without going over the same area twice.
  6. Wipe the head with a dry or wet tissue afterwards — the device is not waterproof.

Avoid broken skin and active acne, and stop if you see redness, swelling or itching. That guidance comes straight from the official product notice.

8. LG Pra.L Melabeam Toning FAQ

What is the LG Pra.L Melabeam Toning?

It is an at-home LED tone-care device from LG Pra.L, the beauty-device brand of LG H&H (LG Household & Health Care) in Korea. It launched in Korea in June 2026 and is listed under model code SPD-003. A straight, pen-shaped body sends three LED wavelengths through a convex lens at the tip.

Which wavelengths and modes does it have?

Yellow 590 nm, red 630 nm and infrared (IR) 830 nm LEDs. TONING mode runs all three LEDs plus electroporation (EP), GLOW mode runs red LED with microcurrent (MC), and RELIEF mode runs red and IR LEDs for a calming step. Depending on the mode there are up to five intensity levels.

Is it a medical device or a laser?

No. LG Pra.L's official product notice states it is not a medical device, and the brand's FAQ explains that it does not work like an in-clinic laser toning treatment. For melasma or a diagnosed pigment condition the brand itself recommends seeing a dermatologist.

How do I use it and how often?

After cleansing, prep with a toner pad and apply a skincare product. Always start at level 1. Glide the head over each area for about 5 minutes. The official guide says once a day for about 5 minutes, using one or two modes at most per day, without overlapping areas.
