1. The Whoo Gongjinhyang Royal 3-Piece Set Review: Overview

This The Whoo Gongjinhyang Royal 3-Piece Set review looks at one of Korea's most popular luxury skincare gift sets. The History of Whoo (더후) is a luxury brand from LG Household & Health Care that reworks Korean royal-court beauty traditions for modern skincare. Gongjinhyang (공진향) is its core line.

The Korean listing we checked is a gift box: a full-size toner (170ml) and emulsion (130ml), plus a 20ml cream, a 10ml essence and a 40ml cleansing foam. It comes wrapped in a dancheong-pattern bojagi (a traditional Korean wrapping cloth), and in Korea it's a classic gift for parents and for holidays.

The Whoo says the line is built on Gong Jin Solution™, an herbal complex based on research into Gongjindan, a traditional royal herbal prescription. The brand pitches it as elasticity care. That's a brand claim, and we haven't tested it ourselves.

3. How to Use the Gongjinhyang Set

✨ Gongjinhyang Morning & Night Routine

  • Step 1 — Cleansing Foam (40ml): Lather with water, wash gently, and rinse well.
  • Step 2 — Toner (170ml): Pour a little into your palm and pat over your face.
  • Step 3 — Essence (10ml): Smooth a small amount over your face.
  • Step 4 — Emulsion (130ml): Spread evenly and pat with your palms until it sinks in.
  • Step 5 — Cream (20ml): Finish with a thin layer, especially at night.

6. The Whoo Gongjinhyang FAQ

What is in The Whoo Gongjinhyang Royal 3-Piece Set?

The Korean listing we reviewed contains a 170ml toner, a 130ml emulsion, a 20ml cream, a 10ml essence and a 40ml cleansing foam, packed in a dancheong-pattern bojagi (traditional Korean wrapping cloth) gift wrap.

How do I use the Gongjinhyang set?

Cleanse with the foam, then apply the toner, the essence, the emulsion and finish with the cream. Use a small amount of each and pat it in gently.

What is Gong Jin Solution?

According to The Whoo, Gong Jin Solution is the brand's herbal complex, developed from research on Gongjindan, a traditional royal herbal prescription. Its elasticity benefits are a brand claim.
