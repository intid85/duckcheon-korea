3. How to Use the Toner, Emulsion and Firming Cream

  1. Cleanse and pat the face lightly dry.
  2. Toner: press a small amount into the skin with your palms. The toner is meant to be the hydrating first layer, not a wipe-off step.
  3. Emulsion: a light layer next. It sits between the watery toner and the richer cream.
  4. Firming Cream: finish with the cream, pressing it in rather than rubbing, especially around the cheeks and jawline.
  5. Morning: follow with sunscreen. At night, the cream can be the last step.

Our editor notes from the Korean write-up describe the toner as fresh and hydrating, the emulsion as a snug, neat finish and the cream as the standout — moisturised skin with a healthy-looking sheen and a mild scent that does not feel heavy morning or night. Sensitivity and texture preferences vary, so treat that as one user's experience, not a clinical result.
